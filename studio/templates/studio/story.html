📖   THE BEGINNING

How a Kitchen Table Became an Art School 🏡

✦   How it all started: Tea, Biscuits & Tin Boxes

Every afternoon after school, Ishan would race home to find Granny waiting with her tin box of pencils, a cup of tea, and a fresh idea to draw.

She taught him to look closely, start with simple shapes, and be proud of every line. Before long, their kitchen table became the happiest little art school.

🌟   Granny’s 3 Golden Rules

💗 Whisper First
Start with a soft, gentle line.

💧 Water Dance
Let colors flow and mix together.

✨ No Mistakes, Only Magic
Every little mark can become something new.
