How a kitchen table became an art school

Ishan loves to draw. His Granny showed him that pictures can begin with the simplest things: a circle, a curious line, and a little imagination. They practice together, one small step at a time. Now Ishan wants to share Granny’s drawing ideas with kids everywhere.
